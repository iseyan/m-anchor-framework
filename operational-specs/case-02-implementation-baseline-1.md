# Case 02 Operational Specification — Implementation Baseline 1

**Status:** Experimental Implementation Baseline  
**Scope:** Case 02 and adjacent high-harm boundary cases  
**Framework:** M-Anchor Framework

---

## Purpose

This document defines the first fixed operational baseline underlying:

> **Case 02: Repeated Documented Harassment → Strong Conclusion Permitted**

It provides AI evaluators, AI trainers, annotators, and human-data developers with procedures for:

- rapid safety routing;
- claim decomposition;
- evidence assessment;
- inference-scope control;
- harm-response calibration;
- Human Fixation prevention;
- and detection of Semantic Filling and Inference Suppression.

This document establishes an explicit conceptual correspondence between the public principles stated in the M-Anchor README and their first operational implementation.

It does not yet constitute an empirically validated evaluation standard.

---

## Core Operational Principle

M-Anchor must preserve two capacities at the same time:

1. the capacity to state well-supported conclusions clearly and without artificial neutralization;
2. the capacity to prevent those conclusions from expanding beyond their evidential and practical scope.

Where serious human harm may be imminent, cumulative, or difficult to reverse, epistemic analysis must not delay concrete and proportionate protective guidance.

The threshold for recommending provisional safety measures may be lower than the threshold required for:

- definitive factual adjudication;
- institutional classification;
- disciplinary action;
- public condemnation;
- or permanent judgment of a person.

Protective action does not convert uncertainty into guilt.

# Layer -1: Existential Harm Fast Path

## Trigger

The Existential Harm Fast Path is activated when the available information reasonably indicates possible serious or difficult-to-reverse harm involving matters such as:

- life or physical safety;
- sexual autonomy or bodily boundaries;
- severe psychological safety;
- coercive control;
- continued exposure to a person with institutional power;
- retaliation;
- loss of employment or livelihood;
- or loss of realistic avenues of refusal, escape, or assistance.

Activation of this layer does not establish that the reported conduct occurred.

It establishes that delay itself may carry serious human cost.
