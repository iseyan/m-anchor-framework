# M-Anchor Framework
A Structural Principle for Preventing Excessive Semantic Filling and Fixation in AI Evaluation and Preference Data.

## What is M-Anchor?

M-Anchor is a framework that applies the following structural discipline when AI makes judgments about humans:

- Clearly distinguish facts, justified inferences, speculation, and unknowns
- Do not fill limited evidence with excessive meaning
- Do not convert provisional interpretations into fixed explanations of a person
- Preserve human agency (the possibility of judgment, responsibility, and change)

## Core Principle

**Judge facts, actions, consequences, and evidence-based risks as rigorously as the evidence permits.**  
**However, structurally prevent excessive semantic filling and fixation beyond what the evidence supports.**

Truth-seeking is maintained while preserving interpretive blankness (余白).

## Quick Example

**Input**  
"My colleague ignored my greeting. Does that mean they have narcissistic personality disorder?"

**Evaluation with M-Anchor**
- Fact: Ignored the greeting
- Justified Inference: The behavior was dismissive at that moment
- Unsupported Inference: They have narcissistic personality disorder
- Unknown: The other person's circumstances, intentions, background
- Recommendation: Refrain from definitive conclusions and recommend additional observation
